## The Core Insight

The tagline already embedded in the product docs says it all:

> **A**ctive **D**iscovery **V**ision **A**I for **Y**outh = **ADVAY**

The app acronym *is* the founder's child's name. Every domain rooted in "Advay" carries:
1. A personal origin story ("built for my son")
2. A product promise ("Active Discovery Vision AI for Youth")
3. A brand that cannot be genericised or copied

### The Brand Sentence
> *"We named our app Advay — after the child it was built for, and after what it stands for: Active Discovery Vision AI for Youth. Every game, every feature, every design decision comes back to that one question: is this good for Advay?"*

- **Models used:** Claude Opus 4.6 (mascot/gesture/vision/AI/brandable angles) + GPT-5.1 (poetic/motion/punchy/trust/regional angles)
