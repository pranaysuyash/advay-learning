## Key Findings Summary

### CV Integration Quality ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)
**All math games demonstrate excellent hand tracking integration:**
- ✅ Hand tracking as primary interaction method (not fallback)
- ✅ Cursor follows finger tip precisely with `GameCursor` component
- ✅ Camera preview visible and functional via `CameraSafeRoute` wrapper
- ✅ Graceful degradation on tracking loss (cursor disappears, game continues)

**Recommendation**: Document this pattern as platform standard for all counting games.

### Scoring System Variants ⚠️ (2 patterns observed)
| Variant | Example Game | Formula | When to Use |
|---------|--------------|---------|-------------|
| **Streak-based** | Counting Objects | `(level × 10) + (streak × 5)` | Games without time pressure |
| **Time-based** | Bubble Count | `100 + (timeRemaining × 5)` | Time-pressure mechanics |

**Recommendation**: Standardize on streak-based formula for consistency, or clearly document when to use each variant.

### Unique Mechanics Identified 🔧
| Game | Unique Feature | Impact | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|--------|----------------|
| **Bubble Count** | 30-second timer per round with time-based scoring | Adds engagement but may stress younger children | Consider optional "relaxed mode" toggle |
| **Counting Collectathon (3D)** | Physical movement + counting | High energy, good for active learners | Ensure safe play space recommendations |
